Hanyang University · Energy Engineering · Battery Engineering

Designing the materials behind a sustainable energy future.

Down at the sub-micron scale, a material’s physicochemical and electrochemical properties decide what an entire device can do. Our lab studies and develops those materials — grounded in materials synthesis. Flagship areas are advanced electrode materials for Li-ion & next-generation batteries and the synthesis of energy storage & conversion materials, with active interests in structural analysis, battery recycling, and battery device engineering. We care about making the planet greener, and about engineering that reshapes how people live.
